American Butter Pie

Main home page

Ingredients

Crust

  • 1 single deep dish pie crust, homemade or store-bought
  • 1 large egg white
  • 1/2 teaspoon water

The ameircan pie filling

  • 3/4 cup firmly packed brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 3/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 1/8 teaspoon ground allspice
  • 1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted and cooled slightly
  • 2 large eggs, at room temperature
  • 1 large egg yolk
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cups apple butter, homemade or store-bought, at room temperature
  • 3/4 cup heavy cream, at room temperature

Direction for baking the pie

Step 1

Roll out pie crust and fit into a 9-inch pie pan. Crimp edges as desired.

Step 2

In a small bowl, whisk together egg white and water until combined. Brush egg white mixture over the entire inside and edge of crust. Place pie crust in the refrigerator while the oven preheats.

Step 3

Preheat the oven to 425 degrees F (220 degrees C).

Step 4

Whisk brown sugar, flour, cinnamon, salt, nutmeg, and allspice together in a large bowl. Add melted butter, eggs, egg yolk, and vanilla and beat until mixture is completely smooth. Add apple butter and heavy cream, and whisk until incorporated.

Step 5

Place pie crust onto a baking sheet. Carefully pour filling into crust.

Step 6

Bake in the preheated oven for 15 minutes, then reduce oven temperature to 350 degrees F (180 degrees C). Continue to bake until there is only a slight jiggle in the center of the pie, 45 to 55 minutes. If the pie crust begins to get too dark, cover the edges with foil or a crust shield, and continue to bake until the filling is set.

Step 7

Remove pie from the oven and let cool at room temperature for 1 hour, then refrigerate pie for at least 4 hours, but preferably overnight.

Step 8

When ready to serve, remove pie from refrigerator and cut into slices.
